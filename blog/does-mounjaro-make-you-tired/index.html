Does Mounjaro Make You Tired, and How Long Does the Fatigue Last?

October 14, 2026 6 min read By thrive.md Editorial Team
Medical advisor: Agustin Arrieta, MD — Otolaryngologist (ENT), South Florida
Table of Contents

  2. How common is fatigue on tirzepatide?
  3. How long does Mounjaro fatigue last?

  5. What can help with low energy on Mounjaro?
  6. When should you call a doctor about tiredness?
  7. The bottom line

The short answer: Yes, some people feel tired on Mounjaro (tirzepatide). In the Zepbound weight-loss trials, fatigue was reported by 5% to 7% of people on tirzepatide versus 3% on placebo. No FDA label or trial report gives a set duration for this fatigue. Gut side effects, which often drive it, are most common during dose increases and ease over time.

Does Mounjaro cause fatigue?

It can, but the evidence is clearer for Zepbound than for Mounjaro. Both drugs contain the same active ingredient, tirzepatide. Mounjaro is approved for type 2 diabetes. Zepbound is approved for weight loss and obstructive sleep apnea.

The Mounjaro prescribing information lists the side effects that showed up in at least 5% of people in its placebo-controlled diabetes trials. That list covers nausea, diarrhea, decreased appetite, vomiting, constipation, indigestion, and stomach pain. Fatigue is not on it.

Fatigue does appear in a different part of the Mounjaro label. In SURPASS-CVOT, a heart outcomes trial of 6,647 adults with type 2 diabetes who took Mounjaro, 10% reported fatigue. That trial compared Mounjaro with dulaglutide (Trulicity), not with placebo. So the 10% figure does not tell you how much of the tiredness came from the drug itself.

How common is fatigue on tirzepatide?

The best dose-by-dose data come from the Zepbound prescribing information. It pools two 72-week weight-loss trials in adults with obesity or overweight. The label groups asthenia (weakness), fatigue, lethargy, and malaise under one "fatigue" heading.

  • Placebo: 3%
  • 5 mg: 5%
  • 10 mg: 6%
  • 15 mg: 7%

Put plainly, about 2 to 4 extra people out of every 100 reported tiredness on tirzepatide compared with placebo. The rate rose slightly with dose. Dizziness followed a similar pattern: 4% to 5% on tirzepatide versus 2% on placebo.

Most people in these trials did not report fatigue at all. If you feel worn out, though, you are not imagining it. It is a listed, recognized reaction.

How long does Mounjaro fatigue last?

No source we could find gives a clear answer. Neither the Mounjaro nor the Zepbound label reports when fatigue starts or how long it lasts. We also did not find a published trial analysis that tracks fatigue over time.

What the labels do say is about gut side effects. Both labels state that most nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea occurred during dose escalation and decreased over time. Since fluid loss and eating less often sit behind the tiredness, many people find their energy returns as these symptoms settle. That is a reasonable expectation, not a measured fact.

Per the label, Mounjaro starts at 2.5 mg weekly and can go up in 2.5 mg steps after at least 4 weeks on a dose. Each step can bring a fresh round of side effects. See our Mounjaro dosing schedule for how the steps work. If fatigue lasts for weeks at a stable dose, or gets worse, talk with your prescriber rather than waiting it out.

Why can tirzepatide make you tired?

The labels do not explain the cause of fatigue directly. But they document several effects that can each leave you drained.

  1. Eating much less. Decreased appetite was reported by 5% to 11% of Mounjaro users in the placebo-controlled trials, versus 1% on placebo. Fewer calories and less protein can mean less energy, especially in the first months.
  2. Dehydration from gut side effects. In the Mounjaro trials, gut side effects occurred in 37% to 44% of users versus 20% on placebo. The label warns that nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea can lead to dehydration and, in some cases, kidney injury. MedlinePlus lists headache, dizziness, and listlessness among dehydration symptoms.
  3. Low blood sugar. Mounjaro alone rarely causes it. The risk rises when it is combined with insulin or a sulfonylurea such as glipizide or glimepiride. MedlinePlus lists tiredness or weakness as a symptom of low blood sugar.
  4. Low blood pressure. In the Zepbound trials, hypotension occurred in 1.6% of tirzepatide users versus 0.1% on placebo. It was more common in people taking blood pressure drugs and was linked with dehydration.

The low blood sugar numbers matter if you take other diabetes medicines. In one Mounjaro trial with basal insulin, 14% to 19% of tirzepatide users had a blood sugar below 54 mg/dL, versus 13% on placebo. With a sulfonylurea, 9.9% to 13.8% had readings below 54 mg/dL. The CDC defines low blood sugar as below 70 mg/dL and severe low blood sugar as below 54 mg/dL. Our guide to Mounjaro low blood sugar with insulin or a sulfonylurea covers this in detail.

What can help with low energy on Mounjaro?

These steps target the likely causes. They are general measures, not a substitute for your prescriber's advice.

  • Drink enough fluids. The Mounjaro label tells patients to drink fluids to lower the chance of dehydration, especially with nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea.
  • Do not skip meals entirely. Small meals with protein can help keep energy steady when appetite is low. Our Mounjaro diet plan has practical ideas.
  • Check your blood sugar if you take insulin or a sulfonylurea and feel tired, shaky, or foggy. Ask your prescriber whether those doses need to change. The label notes that a lower dose of insulin or sulfonylurea may be needed.
  • Stand up slowly if you feel dizzy, especially if you also take blood pressure medicine.
  • Look at sleep and other causes. Tiredness has many sources, including poor sleep, anemia, thyroid problems, and depression. Mounjaro may not be the cause.

If calorie intake is the issue, tracking it for a few days can show whether you are eating far less than you think. Calories.md covers calorie and energy basics.

When should you call a doctor about tiredness?

Mild tiredness during the first weeks or after a dose increase is common. Some signs, though, need prompt attention.

  • Get emergency care if you have confusion, fainting, a seizure, or cannot stay awake. MedlinePlus advises emergency care if low blood sugar symptoms do not improve after a sugary snack.
  • Call your prescriber right away if nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea does not go away. The Mounjaro label asks patients to report this because of the kidney risk. Passing very little or very dark urine is a sign of severe dehydration.
  • Call your prescriber if you have repeated low blood sugar readings, shakiness, or sweating while on insulin or a sulfonylurea.
  • Book a visit if fatigue lasts several weeks at a stable dose, or comes with weight loss you did not expect, low mood, or shortness of breath.

Persistent or severe stomach pain, sometimes spreading to the back, can signal pancreatitis. The label says Mounjaro should be stopped if pancreatitis is suspected, so get care promptly.

The bottom line

Tirzepatide can cause fatigue, but it affects a minority of people. In the Zepbound weight-loss trials, 5% to 7% reported fatigue versus 3% on placebo. Mounjaro's own placebo-controlled diabetes trials did not list fatigue as a common side effect. No source gives a set duration. Gut side effects tend to fade after dose increases, and energy often follows. Fatigue with persistent vomiting, very little urine, or low blood sugar symptoms needs a call to your prescriber.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it normal to feel tired the day after a Mounjaro shot?

Some people report feeling run down in the days after an injection, especially after a dose increase. The FDA labels do not track fatigue by day of the week, so there is no data on this pattern. Fluids and regular small meals may help. Tell your prescriber if it happens every week and does not ease.

Does a higher Mounjaro dose make fatigue worse?

In the Zepbound weight-loss trials, fatigue rose slightly with dose: 5% at 5 mg, 6% at 10 mg, and 7% at 15 mg, versus 3% on placebo. The differences between doses were small. Your prescriber may hold you at a dose longer if side effects are hard to manage.

Can Mounjaro cause low blood sugar if I do not take insulin?

Low blood sugar is uncommon when Mounjaro is used without insulin or a sulfonylurea. In the 40-week monotherapy trial, no Mounjaro users had readings below 54 mg/dL. The risk goes up when it is combined with insulin or a sulfonylurea.

Is Zepbound fatigue different from Mounjaro fatigue?

Both contain tirzepatide, so the effect on energy should be similar. The difference is in how the labels report it. The Zepbound label lists fatigue as a common side effect with dose data, while the Mounjaro placebo-controlled diabetes data did not.

Should I stop Mounjaro if I feel exhausted?

Do not stop or change your dose without talking to your prescriber. They can check for dehydration, low blood sugar, or other causes first. Get emergency care if exhaustion comes with confusion, fainting, or you cannot stay awake.
