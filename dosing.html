Dosing Guide

Mounjaro Dosing Schedule & Administration

FDA-approved dosing regimen, titration schedule, injection technique, and dose adjustment recommendations for Mounjaro (tirzepatide).

⚠️ Prescription Only

Mounjaro requires a prescription and medical supervision. This guide is for educational purposes only. Your healthcare provider will determine the appropriate dose and titration schedule for you. Never adjust your dose without consulting your doctor.

Standard Dosing Schedule

Mounjaro is injected under the skin once a week. Per the FDA label, the starting dose is 2.5 mg once weekly. If more blood sugar control is needed, the dose goes up in 2.5 mg steps, each after at least 4 weeks on the current dose. The maximum is 15 mg once weekly for adults. Going up slowly lowers the risk of stomach side effects.

Week Dose Purpose
Weeks 1-4 2.5 mg once weekly Starting dose, to lower the risk of stomach side effects
Week 5 onward 5 mg once weekly First step up
After at least 4 weeks on 5 mg 7.5 mg once weekly Only if more blood sugar control is needed
After at least 4 weeks on 7.5 mg 10 mg once weekly Only if more blood sugar control is needed (maximum for children 10 and older)
After at least 4 weeks on 10 mg 12.5 mg once weekly Only if more blood sugar control is needed
After at least 4 weeks on 12.5 mg 15 mg once weekly Maximum adult dose

Your doctor decides whether to step up. Source: Mounjaro prescribing information, DailyMed (label revised 08/2026).

Injection Technique

Inject Mounjaro under the skin once a week, at any time of day, with or without meals.

Injection Sites

  • Abdomen: Inject at least 2 inches away from the belly button.
  • Thigh: Front of the thigh.
  • Back of the upper arm: Only if another person gives the injection.

Rotate injection sites each week.

Which pen or vial you have

Mounjaro comes in three forms, and each one is used differently. Ask which one you will get, and follow the Instructions for Use that come with it.

  1. Single-dose pen: holds one weekly dose. Use it once, then throw it away in a sharps container.
  2. KwikPen: holds 4 weekly doses. Attach a new needle and prime the pen before each weekly injection. Never share a KwikPen with another person, even if you change the needle.
  3. Vial: draw the dose with a syringe. Single-dose vials hold one dose; multi-dose vials hold 4 doses.

Missed Dose

If you miss a dose of Mounjaro, the FDA label says:

  • Within 4 days (96 hours): Take the missed dose as soon as possible.
  • More than 4 days: Skip the missed dose and take the next dose on your usual day.
  • Changing your injection day: This is fine if the two doses are at least 3 days (72 hours) apart.

💡 Tip: Set a Weekly Reminder

Choose a consistent day and time each week (e.g., every Sunday morning). Set a phone reminder to help you remember your weekly injection.

Dose Adjustments

The FDA label gives one rule for stepping up: if more blood sugar control is needed, raise the dose by 2.5 mg after at least 4 weeks on the current dose, up to 15 mg once weekly for adults. Your doctor decides when to step up, or whether to stay on your current dose.

⚠️ Hypoglycemia Risk

When Mounjaro is used with insulin or insulin secretagogues (like sulfonylureas), the risk of hypoglycemia increases. Your doctor may reduce your insulin or sulfonylurea dose when starting Mounjaro. Monitor blood glucose closely and report symptoms of low blood sugar (shakiness, sweating, confusion, rapid heartbeat).

Special Populations

Renal Impairment

No dose change is recommended for kidney disease, including end-stage renal disease. Doctors check kidney function when starting or raising the dose in people with kidney problems who have severe stomach side effects, because vomiting and diarrhea can cause dehydration.

Hepatic Impairment

No dose adjustment is needed for patients with hepatic impairment.

Elderly Patients (≥65 years)

No dose change is needed based on age alone.

Pediatric Use

Mounjaro is approved for type 2 diabetes in children 10 years and older. The maximum dose for children is 10 mg once weekly. The KwikPen is not recommended for children to inject themselves.

Storage & Handling

  • All forms: Store in the refrigerator at 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C). Do not freeze, and do not use Mounjaro that has been frozen. Keep it in the original carton to protect it from light.
  • Single-dose pen or single-dose vial: If needed, it can be kept unrefrigerated, up to 86°F (30°C), for a total of 21 days. Throw it away after 21 days at room temperature.
  • KwikPen or multi-dose vial, unopened: Keep it in the refrigerator and use it until the expiration date. If kept at room temperature (up to 86°F / 30°C), throw it away after 30 days.
  • KwikPen or multi-dose vial, opened: Once opened, keep it in the refrigerator or at room temperature up to 86°F (30°C). Throw it away after 30 days or after 4 weekly doses, even if medicine is left.
  • Needles: Remove the needle after each KwikPen injection and never store the pen with a needle on.
